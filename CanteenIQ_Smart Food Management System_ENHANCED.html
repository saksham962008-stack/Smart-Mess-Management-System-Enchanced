Responsible Design

What this system does — and doesn't — claim

What our system can do
  • Measure selected physical parameters
  • Monitor configured thresholds
  • Provide operational data
  • Support future analytics
What it does not claim
  • Temperature alone does not prove food safety
  • Sensor readings do not certify freshness
  • Future donation workflows require safety verification
